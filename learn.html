Learn one thing

Short, practical tips. Each one is a sentence of explanation and one clear action. Do one today, tick it off, come back for another.

  1. Turn on automatic updates

    Updates are mostly security fixes wearing a boring costume. The week a flaw becomes public is the week attackers start using it — updated devices are already patched.

    Tonight: open your phone’s settings, find Software Update, and switch on “download and install automatically”.

  2. Use a password manager

    Nobody can memorise 60 strong, different passwords — so everybody reuses a few weak ones. A manager remembers the strong ones for you; you remember one master password.

    Pick a reputable manager, then change your email password first — it’s the master key to everything else.

  3. Switch on two-factor authentication (2FA)

    A password can be guessed or leaked. A second factor — a code or approval on your phone — means a stolen password alone still isn’t enough to get in.

    Enable 2FA on your email and social accounts today. Prefer an authenticator app over SMS codes when offered.

  4. Back up the files you’d cry over

    Phones get lost, laptops die, files get deleted. A backup turns a disaster into an inconvenience — but only if it exists before you need it.

    Copy your most important folder to a cloud drive AND a USB stick today. Then open the cloud copy to prove it works.

  5. Restart your router properly, monthly

    Routers are small computers that run for months without a break; memory fills and small faults pile up. A monthly restart clears them and installs a fresh connection.

    Unplug the router for 10 full seconds, plug it back in, and give it two minutes. Note the date — repeat monthly.

  6. Slow down for urgent messages

    Every scam shares one ingredient: hurry. “Act now”, “last chance”, “account closing” — urgency is manufactured to stop you checking.

    Next urgent message you get: do nothing for 10 minutes, then verify through the official app or website — never the message’s own link.
